Wektor: privacy policy

The app “Wektor: Math & Physics” (Android) · Last updated: 2026-09-24 · Wersja polska

In short: Wektor works without the internet, without an account, without ads and without analytics. We do not collect, send or share any of your data. Your learning progress is stored only on your phone.

1. Who is responsible for the app

The data controller and publisher of the app is Hubert Haznar (sole proprietorship, Polish tax ID NIP 8393195788), who publishes apps under the studio name Atomic Vulcanus.
Contact: atomicvulcanus@gmail.com

2. What data is processed

The app does not collect personal data and does not send any data to our servers or to third parties. It does not use the advertising ID, analytics tools or tracking services.

Only the following is stored on your device, in the app's storage:

  • learning progress (completed lessons, results, points earned, day streak, test history),
  • settings (chosen learning path, daily goal, lesson length, reminder time, language).

This data never leaves your phone. You can delete it by uninstalling the app, clearing its data in the Android settings or using the “Reset progress” option in the app.

3. Progress backup

The app lets you copy your progress as a text code, e.g. to move it to another phone. The code is created on your device and is not sent anywhere; you decide where to keep it. We have no access to it.

4. Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone; we do not use notification servers. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Android settings.

5. Purchases

If the app offers unlocking the full version, the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive card details or any other payment data. Google only tells the app whether the purchase has been made. Google's data processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Contacting us

If you send us an e-mail (e.g. to report a problem with a task), we process your e-mail address and the content of the message only to reply and to improve the app. We do not share them with anyone. We delete messages when they are no longer needed to handle the report.

The “Report a problem with this task” link in the app opens your e-mail app with a prepared message: the topic of the task, its code (which lets us recreate the same task), your answer, the app version and the language. The app does not send anything by itself — you send the message from your own e-mail account and can edit it before sending.

7. Children

The app is intended for secondary school and university students. Since it collects no data, it does not process children's data either.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to rectify it, to have it erased and to restrict its processing. In practice this applies only to e-mail correspondence with us, because we hold no other data. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts processing data in a different way, we will update this page before the change takes effect. The current version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

For privacy matters, write to:
📧 atomicvulcanus@gmail.com
